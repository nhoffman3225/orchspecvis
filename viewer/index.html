      Getting Around

      The 3D spectrum (top) and the 2D pane (middle) show time × pitch × loudness; the score's notes are outlined in part colours. Drag the 3D view to orbit, wheel to zoom. In the 2D pane: wheel stretches pitch, Shift+wheel pans, double-click resets; hover for time, pitch, level, bar and beat.

      The views (Score, Tutti, Registers, Piano) open below the toolbar, which stays usable. Drag the top edge of a view to see the spectrum above it; drag the bars between panels to resize them (double-click resets).

      Toolbar groups with a coloured tag (Spectrum, Surface, Score, Keys) fold away: click the tag to slide the settings open.

      orchspec

      Score-aware orchestral spectral visualizer. Local-only: nothing leaves this computer. Released under the MIT licence.
