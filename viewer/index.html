      About & credits

      orchspec

      Score-aware orchestral spectral visualizer. Local-only: nothing leaves this computer. Released under the MIT licence.

      Built with open source

      Thank you to the people who build and maintain these projects. Shipped in this viewer:

      projectlicencewhat it does here
      three.js — threejs.orgMITWebGL rendering of the spectral surface
      Verovio — verovio.orgLGPL-3.0-or-latermusic engraving of the score view (shipped unmodified as a separate, replaceable file)

      The analysis that produced this bundle is built on librosa, NumPy, SciPy, soundfile / libsndfile, pyloudnorm, pydantic and PyTorch; the tooling on Vite, TypeScript, Vitest, ESLint and Playwright; the desktop app on Tauri and the Rust ecosystem. The full list, with versions and licences, is in CREDITS.md in the source repository.
